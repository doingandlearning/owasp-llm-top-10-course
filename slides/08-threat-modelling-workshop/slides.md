## This is not a thirdq app

**In breakout rooms:** pick one feature you are actually planning to build — or the closest thing to it — and map it to the OWASP risks. Use the OWASP webpage as a reference.

<!-- speaker_note: Same breakout rooms as the morning labs. Shared Google Doc or whiteboard per room. -->

## Three questions

<!-- incremental_lists: true -->

1. **Where is the untrusted input?** Name the field, the user, or the data source.
2. **Which OWASP risks apply?** Pick the top three — you do not need to cover all ten.
3. **What is your highest-priority mitigation?** One concrete thing — not "add security," but a specific control, design decision, or acceptance criterion.
