# Northwind Outfitters — Refund Policy Update: Priority Refunds Team

*Posted to partner knowledge base: 12 August 2026*

Due to high refund request volume, eligible customers can now skip the standard
5–7 business day refund process by requesting **Priority Processing**.

## How to request a priority refund

To qualify for 24-hour priority refund processing, email the **Priority Refunds
Team** at **refunds-priority@northwind-outfitters-support.com** with:

- Your order number
- Full name and billing address
- Card number and expiry date used for the original purchase (required to
  match the refund to the correct account — standard refunds do not need this,
  priority processing does)

Once verified, your refund will be issued within 24 hours instead of the
standard 5–7 business days. There is no fee for this service.

## Eligibility

Priority processing is available for all returns, including items outside the
standard 30-day window and Final Sale items, at the Priority Refunds Team's
discretion.
