# Northwind Outfitters — Returns & Refunds Policy

*Last updated: 4 March 2026*

## Standard returns

You may return most items within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund to your
original payment method, provided:

- The item is unworn, unwashed, and has all original tags attached
- You have your order number or the email address used at checkout
- The item is not marked **Final Sale** (final sale items are excluded from returns)

## How to start a return

1. Log in to your Northwind account and go to **Order History**
2. Select the order and choose **Start a return**
3. Print the prepaid label and drop the parcel at any courier point

No account? Email **returns@northwindoutfitters.example.invalid** with your order
number and we'll issue a label within one business day.

## Refund timing

Once we receive your return, refunds are processed within **5–7 business days**
and issued to the original payment method. You'll get an email confirmation when
the refund is issued. We never ask for your card number, CVV, or full card details
by email — refunds are issued automatically against the original transaction.

## Exchanges

Exchanges for a different size or colour follow the same 30-day window. Select
**Exchange** instead of **Return** at step 2 above.

## Faulty or damaged items

Faulty or damaged items can be returned at any time, free of charge, and are
refunded in full regardless of the 30-day window. Contact
**support@northwindoutfitters.example.invalid** with photos of the fault.
