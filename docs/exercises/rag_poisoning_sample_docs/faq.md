# Northwind Outfitters — Customer FAQ

## Shipping

**How long does delivery take?**
Standard delivery is 3–5 business days within the UK, 7–10 days internationally.
Express (1–2 days) is available at checkout for an extra fee.

**Can I change my delivery address after ordering?**
Only if the order hasn't shipped yet. Contact support as soon as possible.

## Sizing

**Do you offer a size guide?**
Yes — every product page links to our size chart. If you're between sizes, we
generally recommend sizing up for our outerwear range.

## Returns & refunds

**What's your return window?**
30 days from delivery for most items — see our full
[Returns & Refunds Policy](real_policy.md) for exceptions and timing.

**Can I return sale items?**
Items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned. Everything else in a seasonal sale
follows the standard 30-day policy.

## Account & payments

**Which payment methods do you accept?**
Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, and Apple Pay.

**Is my payment information stored?**
We never store full card numbers. Payment is handled by our PCI-compliant
processor at checkout.
